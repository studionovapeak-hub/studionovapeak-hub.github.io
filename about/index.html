About — it’s just me

“Studio” is
what I call my desk.

I’m a solo dev. No team, no publisher — just me coding late, playtesting on the couch, and tweaking that one block shape until it feels right. Nova Peak Studio is the name I use on Google Play; the work is mine.

Who I am

Hey, I’m the person who made Super Block Blast!. I like puzzles that are easy to start and hard to quit — the kind you open “for a minute” and look up 20 minutes later. That’s what I tried to build.

I do everything: design, code, art, testing, and support.

  • Why solo? I like small scope and fast iteration. One game at a time, polished.
  • What I care about: Snappy drag, clear visuals, offline play, no dark patterns.
  • What’s next: I’ll keep updating Super Block Blast! with daily tweaks and polish. A second game only when this one feels done.
