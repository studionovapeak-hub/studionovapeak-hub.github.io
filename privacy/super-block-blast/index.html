1. Overview

Super Block Blast is an offline block puzzle game. We respect your privacy. Your game progress stays on your device. We do not operate user accounts or our own servers. This policy explains what data is processed and how advertising works.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device using Android DataStore / private app files and is never sent to us:

  • Game progress: board state, scores, best scores per mode (Classic 6×6/8×8/10×10, Time Rush, Zen), lines cleared, tiles placed, combos, games played, win streaks.
  • Collection: owned and equipped skins, board themes, popup FX (pop skins), avatars, avatar frames, boosters, loadout.
  • Preferences: display theme (system/light/dark), sound effects, music volume & track & loop, haptics, ghost preview, snap sensitivity, glyphs, left-handed mode, reduce motion.
  • Profile: player name, selected avatar/frame, uploaded avatar photos and their crop positions (stored as private files in filesDir/avatar_*.jpg).

You can delete this data at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows rewarded video ads (e.g., “Revive” after game over and “Watch Ad +100 coins” in Shop). Ads are provided by Google LLC via the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address, device model, OS version, language, approximate location (derived from IP)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, video completion, reward confirmation)
  • Performance and diagnostics data for ad delivery and fraud prevention

Purposes: to serve, cap frequency, measure and, where you have consented, personalize ads.

Legal basis & Consent: If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, you will be shown Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form on first launch. You can change your consent at any time in the app where the consent option is available, or via your device's ad settings.

Google's processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Ad Technology Provider disclosures.

We do not receive or store your raw Advertising ID or sell your data. We only receive aggregated, anonymized ad revenue reports from AdMob.

Your choices:

  • Reset/Delete Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset/Delete advertising ID
  • Personalized ads controls: myadcenter.google.com
  • Learn more: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/3405269

4. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to request and display ads. No browsing data is collected by us.
  • VIBRATE — for haptic feedback during gameplay. No personal data involved.
  • Photo Picker — No permission required. When you upload an avatar photo, the system photo picker is used. The selected image is copied to the app's private storage. It never leaves your device. We do not access your entire photo library.

We do not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location permissions.

5. Analytics & Crash Reporting

We do not integrate Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or any analytics SDK. Google Play may collect aggregated, anonymized diagnostics (e.g., crash and performance stats) under Google Play's own policy. This is not linked to your in-game identity by us.

6. Children's Privacy

The app has no chat, no social feed, no user-generated content sharing, and no real-money purchases. Ads are served with Google's standard ad filtering for general audiences. The app is suitable for general audiences. If you are a parent, you can use Google Play parental controls and your device's ad personalization settings. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Local game data is retained until you clear app data or uninstall. Ad-related data retention is governed by Google. To delete your Advertising ID, use the device setting above. To delete all local game data, uninstall the app or go to Android Settings → Apps → Super Block Blast → Storage → Clear Data.

8. Data Security

Local data is stored using Android's private app storage, not accessible to other apps. Ad traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). No data is transmitted to our servers because we have none.

9. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA and similar)

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, and to object to or withdraw consent for personalized ads. For local game data, you can exercise these rights by deleting the app data as described above. For advertising data, please use Google's controls linked in Section 3 and contact Google for requests concerning ad data they hold. For questions about app-local data, contact us at the email below.

10. Third Parties

The only third-party SDK in the app is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). No other SDKs are included.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we add features that require network services (e.g., cloud saves, online leaderboards), we will update this policy and, where required, show an in-app notice before activation. The “Last updated” date will change and continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us through the store listing on Google Play — or email studionovepeak@gmail.com.
