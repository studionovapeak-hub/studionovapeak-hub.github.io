Super Block Blast!

Drag colorful blocks onto the board, clear rows & columns, stack combos and chase the blast. Daily challenge + endless. Built for one-handed, five-minute sessions that turn into twenty.

Why this game?

I wanted a puzzle that feels like popping bubble wrap. No timers, no energy, just “one more board.” I playtested it on the bus until strangers asked what I was playing — that’s when I knew it was ready.
