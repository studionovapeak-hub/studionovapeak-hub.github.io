Super Block Blast!

Drop, clear, blast — the puzzle that won't let go.

A 10×10 board, a handful of block shapes, and that *pop* when you clear four lines at once. I made it for 5-minute breaks that turn into 30. No energy, no timers — just combos that stack if you plan ahead.

About the game — from my notes

I kept the rules tiny on purpose. No power-ups that play the game for you. The fun is in the little dilemma: take the easy clear now, or hold out for the big blast? Ads, if enabled, never interrupt a board — only between sessions. See privacy page for the exact AdMob setup.
